01 / The problem

You can study interview questions for weeks and still struggle when someone actually asks you one.

Reading answers and revising technical concepts can help with knowledge, but an interview also asks you to explain that knowledge clearly and respond in the moment.

Getting repeated interview-style practice with useful feedback can be difficult—especially when you're preparing on your own.

The idea

Don't just collect more interview questions. Practice answering them.

02 / Proposed solution

A place to rehearse the conversation, not just memorize the answer.

InterviewReady proposes using AI to simulate interview-style questions and help students identify areas they may want to improve.

The concept is designed around practice: answer a question, review feedback, reconsider your response, and try again before the interview that actually counts.

03 / What it could help with

Three reasons to practice before the real conversation.

01

Practice

Rehearse realistic questions.

Move beyond reading question lists by practicing how you would actually explain an answer in an interview-style setting.

02

Reflect

Notice where answers need work.

Use proposed feedback to spot areas worth revisiting, such as clarity, structure, technical explanation, or incomplete reasoning.

03

Repeat

Try the answer again.

Revisit questions after reflection so that improvement comes from repeated practice rather than memorizing a perfect script.
