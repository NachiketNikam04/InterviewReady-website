Core Overview

Explore the proposed practice model.

InterviewReady reimagines interview prep from passive question study into repeated conversational practice. Explore each pillar of the platform below.

01 / The Problem 01

Knowledge without articulation.

Studying interview questions for weeks doesn't guarantee you can articulate answers clearly and respond in the moment when someone actually asks you one.

Read the Problem →
02 / Proposed Solution 02

Rehearse, reflect, and refine.

InterviewReady proposes using AI to simulate realistic interview scenarios, providing immediate feedback so you can reconsider your approach and try again.

View the Concept →
03 / Key Benefits 03

Built for repeatable gains.

Gain structured practice, spot answers that lack clarity or reasoning, and build authentic confidence through repetition rather than memorized scripts.
