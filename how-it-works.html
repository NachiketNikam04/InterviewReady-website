Step-by-step walkthrough

How InterviewReady works.

Rehearsing for interviews should be systematic, actionable, and repeatable. Here is how students progress from initial practice to polished delivery.

01 Setup

Select Your Role & Focus

Choose your specific domain—such as Software Development, Product Management, or Analytics. Define the interview focus (behavioral, technical explanation, or system design) tailored for final-year placement rounds.

Targeted scenario matching
02 Rehearsal

Converse in Real Time

Engage in a live conversational prompt. The AI interviewer poses dynamic questions and follow-ups. You speak or formulate your thoughts on the spot, replicating the real pressure of live interviews.

Live verbal & structured response
03 Feedback

Review & Re-attempt

Receive instant, objective critique across answer structure, clarity of reasoning, and technical precision. Reconsider weak spots and immediately re-attempt the answer to build muscle memory.

Objective reflection loop

Deliberate Practice

Practice answering questions, not just reading them.

Most preparation fails because candidates prepare in isolation without conversational friction. InterviewReady transforms passive preparation into proactive rehearsals that simulate the exact cadence of top recruiters.

Core Metric

Clearer structure in under 3 sessions.

By isolating weak explanations immediately after you answer, your delivery becomes sharper before you ever face a hiring manager.
