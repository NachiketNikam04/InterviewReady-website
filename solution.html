02 / Proposed Solution

A place to rehearse the conversation, not just memorize the answer.

The idea

Don't just collect more interview questions. Practice answering them.

InterviewReady proposes using AI to simulate interview-style questions and help students identify areas they may want to improve.

The concept is designed around practice: answer a question, review feedback, reconsider your response, and try again before the interview that actually counts.

Rehearsal Loop

01. Answer

Respond in real time

02. Review

Analyze feedback

03. Reconsider

Reflect & refine

04. Try Again
